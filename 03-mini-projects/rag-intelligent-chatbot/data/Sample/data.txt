Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

1. What is this product and who is it for?
Our product is a cloud-based task management and collaboration tool designed for individuals, small businesses, and enterprise teams.

2. How do I create an account?
Simply visit our website, click Sign Up, and fill in your details. You can also sign up using Google or Microsoft accounts.

3. Is there a free trial?
Yes. We offer a 14-day free trial of our Professional plan with no credit card required.

4. What platforms are supported?
Our tool works on web browsers, Android, and iOS. A desktop app is available for Windows and macOS.

5. Can I use it offline?
Yes, the mobile and desktop apps offer offline mode. Your data will automatically sync when you reconnect to the internet.

6. How secure is my information?
We use SSL encryption, multi-factor authentication, and regular security audits to protect your data.

7. How do I reset my password?
Click Forgot Password on the login page, enter your email, and follow the link sent to you to set a new password.

8. Does it support team collaboration?
Yes. You can create teams, assign tasks, share files, and track progress in real time.

9. How do I upgrade or downgrade my plan?
Go to Account Settings → Billing, and choose your preferred plan. Changes take effect immediately.

10. Can I cancel my subscription anytime?
Yes. You can cancel anytime from your account settings, and you won’t be charged for the next billing cycle.

11. Do you offer customer support?
Yes. Our support team is available 24/7 via email, live chat, and phone for Enterprise customers.

12. How do I import my data from another tool?
We provide an Import Wizard that supports CSV, Excel, and integration with major project management tools.

13. Can I integrate this with other apps?
Yes. We integrate with Google Workspace, Microsoft Teams, Slack, Dropbox, and over 50 other services.

14. What payment methods do you accept?
We accept major credit/debit cards, PayPal, and bank transfers for annual subscriptions.

15. Where can I find tutorials and guides?
Visit our Help Center for video tutorials, step-by-step guides, and FAQs.
